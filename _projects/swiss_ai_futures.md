Swiss AI Futures is a national initiative inviting public voices to help shape the future of artificial intelligence in Switzerland. The project is led by a Swiss research consortium and supported by TA-SWISS, the Swiss Foundation for Technology Assessment.

**Swiss AI Futures** is a research project exploring how AI-supported deliberation can combine Maxi-public (large-scale, open public input) and Mini-public (small, representative group deliberation) approaches in a recursive process. The project investigates how public voices and focused group discussions can interact and inform each other, using digital tools and workshops to shape the future of AI policy, work, and education in Switzerland. The resulting report directly informs the Swiss Parliament and national policy discussions.

This initiative is led by a Swiss research consortium and supported by TA-SWISS, with partners including ETH Zurich, University of Zurich, University of St.Gallen, LUT University, and University of Lausanne. The project brings together expertise in computational social science, public policy, education, and ethics.
