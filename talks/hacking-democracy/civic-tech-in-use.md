# Swiss public tech and OmaStadi

Checked 6 September 2026. Replaces Rahvaalgatus and FixMyStreet in the talk.

OmaStadi remains the international reference Josh knows. Helsinki recorded 31,103 voters, a €10 million allocation and 54 selected proposals in 2026. https://paatokset.hel.fi/en/case/hel-2026-004390?decision=b7f23e87-e7f8-4607-9900-90776b1b6764

VelObserver is the bicycle project in the Prototype Fund Switzerland catalogue, round 2. Cyclists rate route segments; the platform aggregates subjective assessments and aims to track improvements. Its public site lists Zurich, Basel, Bern, Gossau, Aarau, Winterthur and Limmattal. The Bern view displayed 161 contributors and 9,012 ratings when checked; these are local cumulative counts, not nationwide reach. https://prototypefund.opendata.ch/en/projects/ and https://velobserver.ch/

The Swiss map is an illustration, not a route map or deployment screenshot. A useful project could connect residents’ experience to an existing planning or monitoring process. Ratings alone do not guarantee infrastructure changes.

Carbon Copy is the Zurich-based association behind @gora. The current product combines learning material, different perspectives, polls and discussion. Its field notes document the Swiss Bevölkerungsrat 2025, Swiss AI Futures and school projects. These are provider-reported deployments, not independent evidence of effectiveness or a large unique-user count. https://www.atgora.org/ and https://www.atgora.org/field-notes

ZüriCityGPT is the likely Zurich GPT reference. Liip’s public OSS version answers questions using Zurich website content and open-source models. It explicitly states it is not associated with the City of Zurich. This is access-to-information public tech, not a binding democratic decision mechanism. Check answers against official pages; no verified adoption figure is claimed. https://oss.zuericitygpt.ch/ and https://github.com/liip/SwissGovernmentChatbots

Only VelObserver is identified here as Prototype Fund-supported. Do not imply that @gora or ZüriCityGPT received that funding. The illustrations are conceptual, not product screenshots. Sources checked 6 September 2026.
