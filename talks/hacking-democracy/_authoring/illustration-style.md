# Presentation illustration direction

User-selected direction, 6 September 2026: mid-century figurative painting with vintage fashion illustration and naïve modernism.

Use elongated bodies, extremely long necks and limbs, slightly awkward proportions, oversized clothing and hats, sparse anonymous faces, flattened perspective and interlocking matte shapes. Define forms with thin, loose, imperfect dark contours. Use subtle dry-brush variation, worn gouache texture and occasional visible brush marks. Keep compositions sparse, with empty space and only a few small still-life details. Mood: elegant, intimate, slightly melancholic, strange and theatrical.

Palette: muted olive, tobacco brown, cream, dark bottle green, burnt orange, dusty beige and charcoal. This replaces the initial sample's brighter cobalt and mustard emphasis for generated illustrations.

Avoid photorealism, detailed faces, smooth gradients, glossy digital finish, decorative clutter and embedded text. When transforming source photographs, preserve their essential composition and subjects.

Current sample: `deliberation-figurative-v2.png`, generated with the built-in image tool from the earlier conceptual illustration. This is a style sample, not a documentary depiction of an actual event. Full prompt: `deliberation-figurative-v2-prompt.txt`.

For subsequent images, reuse this brief and the current sample as the style reference, changing the subject and composition as appropriate. The presentation itself has not been changed.
