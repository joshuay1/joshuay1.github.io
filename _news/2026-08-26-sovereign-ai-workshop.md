I’m organizing the [First Workshop on Sovereign AI for Collaborative and Pluralistic AI Ecosystems](https://sovereignai-workshop.github.io/) at ACM HCOMP × Collective Intelligence 2026 near Washington, D.C., on September 27. The goal is to bring the community together around more collaborative and pluralistic AI ecosystems. We’re currently looking for paper submissions, from early ideas and prototypes to full papers and real-world case studies.
